ℹ️ How to use this planner

What it does

The Schedule Planner helps you map out when different types of work will happen on each project — and automatically calculates how long each phase will take based on your estimates.

The pieces

  • Projects — the main jobs (e.g. a renovation)
  • Work Types — the phases within a project (e.g. Framing, Electrical, Tile)
  • Estimates — total hours and number of employees for each work type. The planner calculates days-to-complete automatically.

Step by step

  1. Click a project row to expand it and see its work types.
  2. Enter hours and employees for each work type. A red badge will appear showing how many business days it'll take.
  3. Drag the red badge onto the calendar to set the start date. The required business days will be highlighted in colour.
  4. To reschedule, drag a highlighted cell to a new start date.
  5. To clear a schedule, click the small ✕ next to the badge.

The result

A colour-coded calendar showing what work is happening, when, and for how long — calculated automatically from your estimates.
